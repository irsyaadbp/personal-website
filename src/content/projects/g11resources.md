## Background

G11 Resources required a professional corporate website to communicate its mining exploration activities, projects, governance, and investor information to stakeholders. The site needed to highlight ongoing projects such as the Koonenberry Belt, Bilpa Corridor, Wilandra Corridor, and Wertago Corridor, while maintaining compliance with ASX listing standards.

## Responsibilities

As part of the development team for Anemon Studio, my role focused on:

- Implementing the site architecture and front-end layouts in Elementor.
- Building custom ACF fields to provide flexible content management for different page types (Projects, Board & Management, Corporate Governance, Investor Centre).
- Developing a custom CMS for managing investor reports and announcements with version control.
- Integrating TradingView charts into the Investor Centre for interactive market data visualization.
- Ensuring mobile responsiveness and accessibility across devices.
- Collaborating with designers and project managers to deliver a corporate look aligned with G11's brand identity.

## Architecture & Tech Stack

Custom CMS modules for:

- Investor Centre (announcements, financial reports, governance documents).
- Board & Management directory.

## Mobile-First & Responsiveness

The website was optimized with a mobile-first design approach:

- TradingView integration for real-time and historical chart visualizations.
- Flexible grids and typography scaling from 375px upwards.
- Testing across devices to ensure investor charts, geological maps, and board profiles render seamlessly.
- Adaptive image handling for performance (responsive image sizes, lazy loading).

## Key Features

**Investor Centre:**

- Real-time ASX share price widget.
- Interactive TradingView charts for investors.
- Custom CMS for uploading and managing announcements, governance PDFs, and financial reports.

## Performance Optimization

To ensure fast loading and compliance with web standards, the site was tested using Google Lighthouse. The final scores achieved:

- Performance: 95
- Accessibility: 95
- Best Practices: 100
- SEO: 100

## Challenges & Learnings

One challenge was balancing Elementor's visual flexibility with the client's need for structured, repeatable content across multiple project pages. To address this:

- Built ACF-powered templates, so project managers could easily update geological data, maps, and announcements without touching layouts.
- Streamlined performance by limiting third-party plugins, ensuring faster load times while maintaining editing flexibility.

Another challenge was ensuring compliance with ASX investor communication standards:

- Integrated a real-time share price feed styled consistently across devices.
- Built a custom CMS so that administrators could manage investor reports and announcements with full version control.
- Embedded TradingView charts to provide investors with an interactive, real-time market data experience.
